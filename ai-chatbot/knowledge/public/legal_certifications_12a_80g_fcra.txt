Title: Official Certifications & Registrations: 12A, 80G Approval and FCRA
Organization: International Human Development and Upliftment Academy (IHDUA)
Website: /about

1. Section 12A / 12AB Registration:
• Legal Registration: Registered under Section 12A / Section 12AB of the Income Tax Act, 1961 (Order Form 10AC).
• Unique Registration Number (URN): AAATI3426LE20216.
• Registration Order Date: 22 March 2022.
• Approved Assessment Years: AY 2022-23 through AY 2026-27.
• Status: Recognized as a non-profit charitable institution dedicated to rural development, education, and welfare.

2. Section 80G Tax Exemption & Form 10BE:
• Legal Approval: Approved under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961 (Order Form 10AC).
• Unique Registration Number (URN): AAATI3426LF20214.
• Approval Order Date: 23 March 2022.
• Approved Assessment Years: AY 2022-23 through AY 2026-27.
• Donor Tax Deduction: Indian individual and corporate donors are entitled to a 50% tax deduction on eligible donations under Section 80G.
• Donation Certificate (Form 10BE): IHDUA files donation records with the Income Tax Department and generates official Form 10BE certificates for donors, enabling seamless tax filing.
• Instant 80G receipts are generated upon successful online donation on the IHDUA website.

3. FCRA (Foreign Contribution Regulation Act) Registration:
• Authority: Registered with the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India.
• Registration Number: 094590127.
• Nature of Registration: Educational and Social.
• Renewal Validity: Renewed with effect from 1 April 2023 for a 5-year validity period (valid through 31 March 2028).
• Foreign Contributions Eligibility: IHDUA is legally authorized to receive foreign donations, grants, and philanthropic contributions for educational and social welfare projects.
• Foreign Donation Remittance: Contributions must be channeled strictly through authorized FCRA banking pathways in compliance with FCRA 2010 rules. For secure wire transfer instructions, international donors should contact the finance team at info@ihdua.org or visit the website donation portal.

4. USA Chapter 501(c)(3) Status:
• Registered non-profit chapter located in Chicago, Illinois.
• Donations made in the United States qualify for 501(c)(3) tax deductions under IRS regulations.

5. Compliance and Restricted Information:
• IHDUA holds valid PAN, 12A, and 80G certificates with all accounts regularly audited by independent Chartered Accountants.
• For security and regulatory compliance, complete internal identification numbers and designated foreign contribution bank account details are shared only through authorized official channels.

6. How to Access or Download Certificates:
• Official copies of registration documents (including 12A/12AB certificate, 80G approval, FCRA certificate, Trust Deed, and Annual Reports) can be accessed under the 'Our Documents' section on the About Us page (/about).
• Donors who have made a contribution receive their 80G tax receipt immediately online. Form 10BE annual donation certificates are issued directly to donors.
• For certified copies, CSR audits, or administrative assistance, donors and partners can write to info@ihdua.org or visit the Contact Us page.
