2. Section 80G approval (Income Tax Act, 1961)
- Issued as Form No. 10AC, Order for approval, under clause (i) of the first proviso to sub-section (5) of section 80G.
- Unique Registration Number (URN): AAATI3426LF20214
- Date of approval: 23-03-2022
- Valid for assessment years AY 2022-23 to AY 2026-27.
- Donations to IHDUA are eligible for deduction under Section 80G. Under the Income Tax Act, donations to such approved institutions qualify for a 50% deduction of the donated amount, subject to the limits set by the Act.
- As a condition of the approval, the certificate of donation is issued to donors in Form No. 10BE (rule 18AB).

3. FCRA registration (Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010)
- Registered with the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India (Foreigners Division, FCRA Wing).
- Registration number: 094590127
- Nature: Educational, Social
- Renewal approved on 09-02-2023; the renewed certificate is valid for five years with effect from 01-04-2023.
- Foreign contributions can be received only in IHDUA's designated FCRA bank account. Account details are shared only through official channels; donors abroad should use the Contact page.

4. PAN and Tax Compliance
- Permanent Account Number (PAN): IHDUA holds a valid PAN registered with the Income Tax Department of India (which forms the base for its 12A and 80G registrations).
- Official 80G receipts and Form 10BE donation certificates are issued to donors for tax filing.
- For security and compliance reasons, IHDUA does not disclose the complete PAN number publicly in chat. Donors receive official documentation with the PAN upon contributing, or can request verified compliance certificates through the Contact Us page.

Official copies of these documents are published in the documents section of the About Us page.
