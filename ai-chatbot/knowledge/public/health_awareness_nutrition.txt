Title: Health Awareness, Nutrition, Anaemia and Hygiene Programmes
Organization: IHDUA
Website: /health-awareness

Overview:
IHDUA’s preventive healthcare interventions focus on breaking cycles of preventable rural illness through community health literacy, maternal and child wellness, and frontline healthcare worker training.

Key Areas of Focus:
• Maternal & Reproductive Health: Interactive educational workshops on antenatal care, safe childbirth practices, institutional delivery, and postpartum wellness.
• Nutrition & Anaemia Prevention: Educating rural women and adolescent girls on iron-rich indigenous diets, green leafy vegetables, balanced child nutrition, and anaemia testing.
• Adolescent & Menstrual Hygiene: Workshops on hygienic practices, dispelling taboos, and providing accessible sanitary napkins.
• Frontline Capacity Building: Specialized training for rural Anganwadi workers and community health volunteers to identify malnutrition and early disease symptoms.

Verified Impact Statistics:
• 160 Reproductive and child health education sessions conducted.
• 855 Women reached in targeted nutrition and anaemia prevention sessions.
• 988 Women engaged through comprehensive hygiene and sanitation sessions.
• 34+ Frontline Anganwadi workers trained.
