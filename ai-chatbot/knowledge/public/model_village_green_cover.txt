Title: Model Village Initiatives and Increase the Green Cover
Organization: IHDUA

Model Village (Gram Swaraj):
Inspired by Mahatma Gandhi's vision of self-reliant villages, IHDUA's Model Village program rejuvenates underserved rural settlements through:
• Total Sanitation: Constructing individual household toilets and modern community sanitation facilities to end open defecation.
• Potable Water: Installing water purification kiosks and rainwater recharging structures.
• Solar Street Lighting: Illuminating village main streets and dark pathways for safety and night study.
• Solid & Liquid Waste Management: Organic composting units and wastewater drainage.

Increase the Green Cover:
• Extensive afforestation campaigns across Karnataka's rural belt.
• Planting thousands of native neem, banyan, peepal, tamarind, and fruit-bearing saplings along village roadsides, school perimeters, and common pastures.
• Engaging school children and youth clubs as 'Green Ambassadors' to nurture trees to maturity.
• Preserving local water tables and mitigating climate risks in drought-prone areas.
