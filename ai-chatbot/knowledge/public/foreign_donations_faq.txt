Title: Foreign and International Donations FAQ (Outside India, USD, Foreign Cards, NRIs)
Source: IHDUA website Donate page
Website: /donate

Do you accept donations in USD or other foreign currencies?
- Yes, donors from outside India (including in USD, GBP, EUR, or other foreign currencies) can contribute to IHDUA through the IHDUA Finance Team.
- Choose "Outside India" on the Donate page (/donate) and email the IHDUA Finance Team at finance.ihdua@gmail.com. They will guide you through the donation process and share the necessary bank details and instructions to complete your contribution.

Why can't I directly make an international donation through the website?
- Online payment gateways on the website (Razorpay) are configured for Indian Rupee (INR) donations from India only, so direct online card payments from outside India are not available on the website.
- For donations from outside India, IHDUA handles contributions directly through the Finance Team at finance.ihdua@gmail.com so donors receive the correct bank details and transfer instructions.

Can I make an international donation using a foreign credit card?
- Online card payments from outside India are not available on the website.
- Donors outside India should choose "Outside India" on the Donate page (/donate) and email the IHDUA Finance Team at finance.ihdua@gmail.com for bank transfer details and instructions.

Can someone living in the USA, UK, UAE or NRIs donate to IHDUA?
- Yes. Donors from any country outside India (including the USA, UK, UAE, and NRIs) should choose "Outside India" on the Donate page (/donate) and email finance.ihdua@gmail.com.

Do I need to provide any additional documents for foreign donations?
- For donations from outside India, please email the IHDUA Finance Team at finance.ihdua@gmail.com. They will guide you through the process and let you know the required bank details, transfer instructions, and any documentation needed to complete your contribution.
- Note that overseas / foreign donations do not carry the Indian Section 80G tax benefit.
