Title: Women Empowerment, Self-Help Groups (SHGs) and Microfinance
Organization: IHDUA

Grassroots Women Mobilization:
• Over 7,578+ rural women organized into 697+ Self-Help Groups (SHGs) across 18 registered Federations.
• The federation acts as an apex democratic structure managing collective savings, emergency loans, and micro-business investments.
• Weekly thrift and credit meetings foster savings habits, transparency, and peer accountability.
• Women develop public speaking skills, financial literacy, and community decision-making confidence, actively contributing to local Gram Panchayats.

Microfinance & Bank Credit Linkages:
• IHDUA collaborates with nationalized, regional rural, and cooperative banks to facilitate institutional credit.
• Over ₹9.14 Crores in loans and credit linkages have been facilitated across 218+ bank credit linkages.
• Collateral-free, low-interest microcredit eliminates the burden of exploitative moneylenders.
• Supported enterprises include dairy farming, poultry, small retail shops, tailoring centers, food processing, and agarbatti manufacture.
