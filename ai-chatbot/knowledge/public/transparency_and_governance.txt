Title: Governance, Transparency & Official Certifications (80G, FCRA, Trust)
Organization: IHDUA
Website: /about

Overview:
IHDUA operates with unwavering dedication to transparency, public accountability, and rigorous regulatory compliance. All programs, expenditures, and outcomes are audited and open to donor inspection.

Key Institutional Certifications:
• Registered Private Trust (India): Established under the Indian Trusts Act as a public charitable institution.
• Section 80G Tax Exemption (India): Valid registration granting 50% tax deduction on donations under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act. Instant 80G receipts are generated.
• FCRA Registration (Foreign Contribution Regulation Act): Officially authorized by the Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India, to accept charitable contributions, grants, and donations from foreign donors and international entities.
• US 501(c)(3) Status: IHDUA's Chicago chapter is registered as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt public charity in the United States.
• Annual Reports & Audited Financials: Annual reports from 2015 through 2026 are archived and available for partners, individual donors, and CSR audit teams.
