Title: Skills & Employment: Stitching Unit, Youth Training and Farmers
Organization: IHDUA

Stitching & Garment Production Unit:
• Over 2,174 women trained in professional tailoring, fabric cutting, garment assembly, and machine maintenance.
• Direct employment opportunities at IHDUA's garment center manufacturing school uniforms, corporate apparel, and reusable shopping bags.
• Independent home micro-tailoring support: Women receive certified diplomas and assistance acquiring sewing machines, giving them consistent monthly incomes.

Youth Skills & Employment:
• Computer & IT Courses: Over 514+ rural youth trained in basic computer hardware, Microsoft Office suite, Tally accounting, and typing.
• Healthcare General Duty Assistants (GDA): Over 267+ youth certified as healthcare and geriatric nursing assistants, securing placements in hospitals and clinics.
• Soft skills, spoken English, and interview preparation workshops.

Farmers Support:
• 43 Farmer Self-Help Groups established.
• Promoting organic farming, bio-fertilizers, vermicomposting, and soil health testing.
• Rainwater harvesting, drip irrigation support, and direct market linkage for fair crop prices.
