Title: About IHDUA
Organization: International Human Development & Upliftment Association
Location: Karnataka, India and Chicago, USA
Website: https://ihdua.org

The International Humanitarian Development & Upliftment Association (IHDUA) is a registered not-for-profit organisation working with rural and underserved communities to improve quality of life through education, healthcare, livelihood support, and sustainable development.
For over two decades, IHDUA has worked at the grassroots level in Karnataka, India, and internationally through its USA chapter in Chicago.

Mission:
To empower rural communities through sustainable and inclusive development initiatives.

Vision:
A just and equitable society where every individual has the opportunity to live a healthy, dignified, and productive life.

Core Values:
• Compassion
• Integrity
• Inclusivity
• Sustainability
• Community Ownership
• Accountability

Key Focus Areas:
1. Education & Learning (rural schools, STEM learning, extracurriculars, scholarships)
2. Women Empowerment & Sustainable Livelihoods (Self-Help Groups, microfinance, federations)
3. Skills & Employment (Stitching Unit, youth computer courses, nursing assistants, farmer training)
4. Model Village (village rejuvenation, sanitation, drinking water, solar lighting, afforestation)
5. Healthcare & Well-being (free medical camps, diagnostic screening, maternal health awareness)
