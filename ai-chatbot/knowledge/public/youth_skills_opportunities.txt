Title: Youth Empowerment, Digital Skills & Vocational Opportunities
Organization: IHDUA
Website: /youth

Overview:
IHDUA’s Youth Skill Development Project equips rural students and youth with essential vocational capabilities, digital literacy, and job readiness so they can access formal employment and launch micro-enterprises.

Core Training Programmes:
• Computer & Digital Literacy Courses: Practical training in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint), basic hardware maintenance, digital communications, Internet research, and typing.
• General Duty Healthcare Assistants (GDA): Intensive, certified training preparing youth to work as nursing care assistants, bedside caregivers, and patient aides in clinics, hospitals, and nursing homes.
• Tailoring & Garment Manufacturing: Vocational courses teaching precision cutting, machine operations, pattern design, and garment stitching.
• Soft Skills & Career Guidance: Communication workshops, spoken English, resume preparation, and interview readiness.

Verified Impact Statistics:
• 514+ Rural youth completed Computer Training courses.
• 265+ (267+) Certified as General Duty Healthcare Assistants (GDA).
• 2,161+ (2,174+) Trainees completed Tailoring courses.
• 3,290+ Total learners recorded across IHDUA's skill development programmes.
