Title: IHDUA Trust Deed: Founding History and Charitable Objectives
Organization: International Human Development and Upliftment Academy Trust (IHDUA)
Website: /about

Organization History & Establishment:
The International Human Development and Upliftment Academy (IHDUA) was established as a registered public charitable trust under the Indian Trusts Act. Founded with the overarching vision of rural empowerment and Gram Swaraj (self-reliant communities), IHDUA has worked for over two decades across Karnataka and internationally through its USA chapter in Chicago.

Founding Charitable Objectives (Trust Deed):
The Trust Deed sets forth the foundational charitable objects across key dimensions of community upliftment:

1. Education:
• Establishing, running, and supporting schools, learning centers, libraries, and laboratories in rural and underserved areas.
• Flagship institutions include Vinayaka Jnana Vidyashale in Mysore and Adarsha Academy in KRS (Krishnarajasagara).
• Providing scholarships, subsidized education, midday nutrition, STEM kits, and digital literacy so no child is left behind.

2. Child Welfare:
• Supporting destitute, orphaned, and vulnerable children with shelter, healthcare, nutrition, and mentorship.
• Fostering holistic child development, safety, and rights protection.

3. Culture & Heritage:
• Preserving, promoting, and celebrating Indian cultural heritage, traditional arts, classical and folk music, literature, and indigenous crafts.
• Supporting rural artisans, weavers, and cultural performances.

4. Social Welfare:
• Providing comprehensive relief and financial/material support to underprivileged individuals, widows, senior citizens, and destitute families.
• Responding to community emergencies and advancing social equity.

5. Health & Medical Welfare:
• Conducting free rural medical camps, diagnostic screenings, vision checkups with free spectacles, pediatric clinics, and cancer screenings.
• Spreading awareness on maternal health, child nutrition, anaemia prevention, and menstrual hygiene in partnership with local Anganwadi workers.

6. Environment & Ecology:
• Promoting afforestation, tree planting (Increase the Green Cover), and wildlife conservation.
• Protecting rural water resources through rainwater harvesting, farm ponds, and desiltation of village tanks.
• Clean energy adoption including solar street lighting in rural hamlets and organic composting.

7. Sports & Physical Education:
• Encouraging rural youth and schoolchildren in athletics, indigenous sports, and team games.
• Providing sports equipment, coaching, and playground facilities for healthy physical development.

8. Skill Development & Livelihoods:
• Conducting vocational training courses including industrial tailoring (Stitching Unit), computer IT applications, and General Duty Assistant (GDA) nursing care.
• Facilitating institutional microfinance bank credit linkages for women-led Self-Help Groups (SHGs) to launch micro-enterprises.

9. Community & Democratic Empowerment:
• Organizing public lectures, seminars, workshops, and exhibitions on social reform, legal literacy, and community ownership.
• Strengthening Gram Panchayats and village collectives under the Gram Swaraj model.

Governance:
The Trust is administered by a dedicated Board of Trustees headed by Founder & Chairperson Dr. B.S. Ajaikumar and Trustee Bhagya Ajaikumar, with all operations governed in strict accordance with the Trust Deed and Indian charitable laws.
