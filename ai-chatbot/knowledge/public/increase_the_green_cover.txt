Title: Increase the Green Cover, Afforestation and Agroforestry
Organization: IHDUA
Website: /increase-the-green-cover

Overview:
IHDUA’s Increase the Green Cover initiative tackles rural environmental degradation, loss of soil fertility, and drought risks through community-driven afforestation, agroforestry learning, and greening of common lands.

Programs & Methods:
• Household Fruit Sapling Distribution: Supplying fruit saplings (such as mango, guava, amla, papaya, and pomegranate) directly to rural families, providing long-term nutrition and supplemental income.
• Agroforestry & Diversified Cultivation: Promoting multi-cropping agroforestry and sunflower cultivation workshops to improve soil fertility and farm resilience.
• Model Village Exposure Visits: Organizing educational exposure tours for village elders, women panchayat leaders, and farmers to witness working model farms and ecological initiatives.
• Green Ambassadors in Schools: Involving students from Vinayaka Jnana Vidyashale and Adarsha Academy KRS in tree plantation and water conservation stewardship.

Verified Impact Statistics:
• 5 Fruit saplings distributed per family across target villages.
• 16 Village participants in Model Village exposure visits.
• 18 Participants from neighboring villages engaged in exposure visits.
• 32 Farmers reached through sunflower cultivation awareness programs.
