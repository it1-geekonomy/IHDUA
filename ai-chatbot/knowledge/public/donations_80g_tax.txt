Title: How to Donate to IHDUA
Source: IHDUA website donation form (how the live donation system works)
Website: /#donate

Donating online
- Donations are made through the Donate form on the IHDUA website. Payments are processed securely by Razorpay; the payment options shown depend on what Razorpay offers for the chosen currency.
- Donors choose an amount and a cause, then enter their details (name, phone and email; Indian donors can also add their PAN and address).
- Donors in India donate in INR. Donors outside India can choose their country and donate in a supported foreign currency.
- After a successful payment, the donor receives a donation receipt by email.

Tax benefit for Indian donors
- Donations from India in INR are eligible for the Section 80G tax benefit. Donors should enter their PAN in the form so it is recorded for the 80G receipt.
- Donations from outside India do not carry the Indian 80G benefit.

One-time and recurring donations
- Donors can choose One Time, Monthly, Quarterly (every 3 months) or Yearly.
- Recurring donations are available only for donors in India paying in INR.
- For a recurring donation, the chosen amount is charged automatically at the chosen frequency after the donor authorises the mandate in the Razorpay checkout.
- To change, pause or cancel a recurring donation, donors can contact IHDUA through the Contact page.
