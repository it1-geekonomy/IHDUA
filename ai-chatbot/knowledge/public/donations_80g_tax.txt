Title: How to Donate to IHDUA
Source: IHDUA website donation form and /donate funding projects page
Website: /donate

Donating online
- Donations are made through the IHDUA website Donate form (/donate or the home-page donation card).
- First step: “Where are you donating from?” — choose India or Outside India.
- India: donate in INR with Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking). Enter name, phone, email, city and address as prompted.
- India PAN and address: for donations of ₹2,000 or more, PAN Card No. and Address are mandatory. Below ₹2,000, PAN is optional; enter PAN to receive an 80G tax receipt.
- Outside India: online payment gateways cannot currently process foreign donations for IHDUA. After choosing Outside India and Donate Now, the site asks the donor to email the IHDUA Finance team at finance.ihdua@gmail.com. Finance shares the process and next steps for contributing from outside India. Donors can also use Contact Us.
- Do not invent or quote FCRA bank account numbers in chat. Direct overseas donors to the Donate form (Outside India) or to email finance.ihdua@gmail.com.
- After a successful Razorpay payment (India), the donor receives a donation certificate by email. Indian donors who provide a valid PAN also receive an 80G tax receipt.
- Overseas / foreign gifts do not carry the Indian 80G benefit. A thank-you certificate may be issued after Finance processes the donation; there is no 80G receipt for foreign donors.

Project-based donations
- Yes, donors can donate project-wise. Open the Donate page at /donate to see IHDUA funding projects seeking support.
- Each project shows a total amount sought and a component-wise funding breakup (line items).
- Donors can:
  1. Choose a specific component (line item) and donate that amount.
  2. Support a full project.
  3. Make a general donation that is not tied to one project component.
- On /donate, use “Browse projects” to review breakups, or “General donation” to give without picking a project line item.
- The list of funding projects (titles, totals, and component breakups) comes from the CMS. Only currently published projects on /donate are real — do not invent project names.
- For details of a named project, use the CMS funding-project content indexed for that project, or direct the visitor to /donate.
- The donation category recorded for a project gift is usually the project title, or “Project title — Component name” when a line item is chosen.

Tax benefit for Indian donors
- Donations from India in INR are eligible for the Section 80G tax benefit when a valid PAN is provided.
- For ₹2,000 or more, PAN and address are required on the form.
- Donations from outside India do not carry the Indian 80G benefit.

One-time and recurring donations
- Donors can choose One Time, Monthly, Quarterly (every 3 months) or Yearly.
- Recurring donations are available only for donors in India paying in INR.
- For a recurring donation, the chosen amount is charged automatically at the chosen frequency after the donor authorises the mandate in the Razorpay checkout.
- To change, pause or cancel a recurring donation, donors can contact IHDUA through the Contact page.
