Title: How to Donate to IHDUA
Source: IHDUA website donation form and /donate funding projects page
Website: /donate

Donating online
- Donations are made through the IHDUA website Donate form (/donate or the home-page donation card).
- First step: “Where are you donating from?” — choose India or Outside India.
- India: donate in INR with Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking). Enter name, phone, email, city; PAN is optional for an 80G receipt.
- Outside India: after Donate Now, the site shows IHDUA’s SBI FCRA account details for wire transfer (not Razorpay). Donors must email info@ihdua.org or use Contact Us with name, contact number, transaction number, amount, email and postal address so the donation can be processed.
- After a successful Razorpay payment (India), the donor receives a donation certificate by email. Indian donors who enter a PAN also receive an 80G tax receipt.
- Overseas donors should complete the bank remittance using the FCRA details shown, then email or contact IHDUA with the required details.

Project-based donations
- Yes, donors can donate project-wise. Open the Donate page at /donate to see IHDUA funding projects seeking support.
- Each project shows a total amount sought and a component-wise funding breakup (line items).
- Donors can:
  1. Choose a specific component (line item) and donate that amount.
  2. Support a full project.
  3. Make a general donation that is not tied to one project component.
- On /donate, use “Browse projects” to review breakups, or “General donation” to give without picking a project line item.
- Examples of projects that may appear (published projects are managed in the CMS and can change): School Essentials; Rural School Transformation; Watershed Development; Organic Farming Programme; Prevention of Child Marriage; Solid Waste Management; Support for Persons with Disabilities; Millet Processing & Food Unit; Edible Oil Processing Unit.
- The donation category recorded for a project gift is usually the project title, or “Project title — Component name” when a line item is chosen.

Tax benefit for Indian donors
- Donations from India in INR are eligible for the Section 80G tax benefit. Donors should enter their PAN in the form so it is recorded for the 80G receipt.
- Donations from outside India do not carry the Indian 80G benefit.

One-time and recurring donations
- Donors can choose One Time, Monthly, Quarterly (every 3 months) or Yearly.
- Recurring donations are available only for donors in India paying in INR.
- For a recurring donation, the chosen amount is charged automatically at the chosen frequency after the donor authorises the mandate in the Razorpay checkout.
- To change, pause or cancel a recurring donation, donors can contact IHDUA through the Contact page.
