Title: Donations, Tax Exemption (80G & 501c3) and Payment Modes
Organization: IHDUA

Tax Benefits:
• India Donors: All contributions to IHDUA qualify for tax deduction under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act (50% deduction on taxable income). An official 80G receipt and certificate are generated and sent to the donor.
• US Donors: Contributions from United States residents are tax-deductible under Section 501(c)(3) through the IHDUA Chicago non-profit branch.

How to Donate:
1. Online Donation: Via the 'Donate' button/form on the website (https://ihdua.org/#donate). Supports UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Net Banking, Debit Cards, and Credit Cards.
2. Sponsorship Categories:
   - Sponsor a child's annual education at Vinayaka Jnana Vidyashale or Adarsha Academy KRS
   - Sponsor a tailoring sewing machine kit for a rural woman graduate
   - Sponsor a free rural health camp or cataract eye surgery
   - Sponsor planting and maintenance of 50 trees under Increase the Green Cover
3. Corporate CSR Partnerships:
   - IHDUA is registered and compliant for Corporate Social Responsibility partnerships under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) guidelines. Companies can collaborate on education, healthcare, and livelihood CSR projects.
