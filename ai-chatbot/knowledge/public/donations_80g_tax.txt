Title: Donations, Tax Exemption (80G & 501c3) and Payment Modes
Organization: IHDUA

Tax Benefits:
• India Donors: All contributions to IHDUA qualify for tax deduction under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act (50% deduction of the donation amount). An official 80G receipt and Form 10BE certificate are generated and sent to the donor.
• US Donors: Contributions from United States residents are tax-deductible under Section 501(c)(3) through the IHDUA Chicago non-profit branch.

How to Donate:
1. Online Donation & Payment Modes:
   - Via the 'Donate' button/form on the website (https://ihdua.org/#donate).
   - Supports UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Net Banking, Debit Cards, and Credit Cards via Razorpay.
2. One-Time vs Recurring Donations (AutoPay):
   - Donors can choose to donate 'One Time' or set up a 'Recurring Donation'.
   - Recurring Frequency Options: Monthly (every month), Quarterly (every 3 months), or Yearly (every year).
   - Recurring donations / AutoPay are currently available for India (INR) donors.
   - The selected amount is automatically donated at the chosen frequency. Donors can pause or cancel recurring donations anytime.
   - Official 80G tax receipts are generated and emailed automatically after each charge.
3. Sponsorship Categories & Causes:
   - General Donation
   - Sponsor a child's annual education at Vinayaka Jnana Vidyashale or Adarsha Academy KRS
   - Sponsor a tailoring sewing machine kit for a rural woman graduate
   - Sponsor a free rural health camp or cataract eye surgery
   - Sponsor planting and maintenance of 50 trees under Increase the Green Cover
4. Corporate CSR Partnerships:
   - IHDUA is registered and compliant for Corporate Social Responsibility partnerships under Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) guidelines. Companies can collaborate on education, healthcare, and livelihood CSR projects.
