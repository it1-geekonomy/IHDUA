Title: When IHDUA Was Established, Focus Areas and Approach
Source: IHDUA official profile (About Us, Home and Milestones)
Website: /about

When was IHDUA established?
- IHDUA was established in 1991 in Mysuru, Karnataka.
- IHDUA is a non-governmental, non-profit organisation, set up under the Indian Trusts Act.
- Milestone 1991: Trust registration.

What is the full name of IHDUA?
- Registered name: International Human Development and Upliftment Academy (IHDUA). Always use this name. Do not expand the acronym in any other way.

Where is IHDUA located?
- IHDUA's headquarters / India office is in Mysuru, Karnataka (IHDUA Center, Mysore–Ooty Road, Mysore, Karnataka 570008, India). IHDUA also lists a US office address in Chicago, IL on the Contact Us page (/contact).

Where does IHDUA work? Does IHDUA operate in every Indian state?
- No, IHDUA does not operate in every Indian state.
- IHDUA primarily operates in the Mysuru and Chamarajanagara districts of Karnataka, including Gundlupet Taluk of Chamarajanagara district (a drought-prone and economically underserved region).

What are the major focus areas of IHDUA?
- IHDUA's major focus areas include:
  - Education (Education for All, rural schools such as Vinayaka Jnana Vidyashale and Adarsha Academy, school essentials and rural school transformation)
  - Healthcare & Well-being (health camps, health education, awareness programmes)
  - Women's Empowerment (Self-Help Groups / SHGs, collective savings, micro-finance initiatives, leadership)
  - Better Livelihoods & Skill Development (tailoring/stitching, vocational and alternative livelihood opportunities)
  - Sustainable Growth & Agriculture (organic farming, farmer support, model village initiatives, watershed development)
  - Community Empowerment & Rural Development (youth development, prevention of child marriage, community participation)

What makes IHDUA different from other NGOs?
- IHDUA is a people-led organisation established in 1991 with over 35 years of grassroots work in rural Karnataka.
- Its approach focuses on community participation, local ownership, and long-term impact — working directly with rural and underserved communities to understand their needs and build practical, self-reliant solutions across education, healthcare, women's empowerment, sustainable agriculture, and livelihoods.

Does IHDUA guarantee employment or provide personal loans?
- No. IHDUA is a rural development non-profit organisation and does not guarantee employment. It supports communities through education, skill development, and livelihood training so people can build self-reliant futures.
- No. IHDUA does not provide personal loans to the general public. In rural communities, IHDUA facilitates women's Self-Help Groups (SHGs) that encourage collective savings and micro-finance initiatives among group members.
