Title: Rejuvenation of Villages & Rural Infrastructure (Gram Swaraj)
Organization: IHDUA
Website: /rejuvenation-of-villages

Overview:
IHDUA’s Village Rejuvenation initiative draws inspiration from Mahatma Gandhi's Gram Swaraj vision of self-reliant, clean, and vibrant village ecosystems. It addresses critical infrastructure deficits in sanitation, drinking water, renewable lighting, and agricultural productivity.

Key Activities & Infrastructure:
• Solar Street Lighting: Installation of durable solar-powered street lights on village roads and dark pathways to ensure nighttime safety for women, children, and evening study.
• Community & Household Sanitation: Construction of modern individual household toilets and community facilities to eradicate open defecation and improve public health.
• Sustainable Agriculture & Livestock: Providing vermicompost units, soil health testing, and nutritious green cattle fodder.
• Potable Water Access: Rainwater recharge pits and filtration kiosks ensuring clean, fluorosis-safe drinking water.

Verified Impact Statistics:
• 16+ Solar Street Lights installed in rural hamlets.
• 14+ People benefited from sanitation toilet construction.
• 48 Farmers participated in soil health and composting training.
• 81+ Cattle supported through green fodder distribution programs.
