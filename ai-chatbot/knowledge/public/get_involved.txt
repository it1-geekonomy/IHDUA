How to get involved
- There is no separate Get Involved website page at /getinvolved. That path is not a real content page.
- On the IHDUA website, “Get Involved” is the yellow section on the Home page: /#get-involved
- Ways to take part:
  1. Donate — open the Donate page at /donate (India via Razorpay; Outside India: email Finance at finance.ihdua@gmail.com for the foreign donation process).
  2. Volunteer — use the Contact Us page at /contact and choose Volunteer Opportunities.
  3. Partner — use the Contact Us page at /contact and choose Partnership & Collaboration.
- Footer “Get Involved” links also point to Donate (/donate) and Home for Volunteer / Partner (scrolls to Get Involved).
