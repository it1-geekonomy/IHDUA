Title: Get Involved with IHDUA: Volunteering, Rural Residency, Partnerships and CSR
Source: IHDUA website Home page Get Involved section and Contact Us page
Website: /#get-involved

How to get involved
- There is no separate Get Involved website page at /getinvolved. On the IHDUA website, "Get Involved" is on the Home page at /#get-involved.
- There are three main paths to get involved:
  1. Donate — open the Donate page at /donate (India via Razorpay; Outside India: email Finance at finance.ihdua@gmail.com).
  2. Volunteer — click "Apply as Volunteer" in the Get Involved section (/#get-involved), or use the Contact Us page (/contact) and choose "Volunteer Opportunities".
  3. Partner — click "Initiate Partnership" in the Get Involved section (/#get-involved), or use the Contact Us page (/contact) and choose "Partnership & Collaboration" or "CSR Projects".

Volunteering and the Rural Residency Programme
- How can I apply for the rural residency programme or become a volunteer?
  - Apply using the "Apply as Volunteer" form in the Get Involved section on the Home page (/#get-involved), or submit an enquiry on the Contact Us page (/contact) under "Volunteer Opportunities".
  - The volunteer application form collects your full name, email, phone number, area of interest, skills, and an optional resume upload.
- What is the rural residency programme and where do volunteers work?
  - IHDUA invites volunteers to apply their engineering, medical, or digital skills directly through its rural residency programme located in Mysore (Mysuru) district, Karnataka.
- Who can volunteer? Can engineering students, software engineers, medical professionals, or international volunteers apply?
  - Yes. Engineering students, software engineers / digital professionals, medical professionals, educators, and other skilled individuals are welcome to apply.
  - International volunteers and people interested in short-duration volunteering can also apply via the "Apply as Volunteer" form (/#get-involved) or Contact Us (/contact) and mention their location, availability, and duration so the IHDUA team can advise on suitable opportunities.
- What skills are required to become a volunteer? What kind of work can volunteers contribute?
  - No single mandatory qualification is listed; IHDUA welcomes engineering, medical, digital, teaching, and community-support skills.
  - Volunteer areas of interest on the application form include:
    - Teaching & Education
    - Healthcare & Medical Camps
    - Community Outreach
    - Women Empowerment
    - Agriculture & Sustainability
    - Youth Development
    - Event Support
    - Design & Digital Marketing
    - Administration & Operations
    - Other

Corporate Partnerships, CSR Funding and Academic Research Partnerships
- Does IHDUA accept CSR funding? Can corporates sponsor educational programmes or complete rural development projects?
  - Yes. IHDUA welcomes Corporate CSR integrations, corporate partnerships, and programme sponsorships — including sponsoring rural education programmes (such as School Essentials or Rural School Transformation) and complete rural development projects (such as Watershed Development or Organic Farming).
- Does IHDUA support academic research partnerships?
  - Yes. IHDUA explicitly supports academic research partnerships and NGO / non-profit collaborations aligning resource networks with village-led proposals.
- Partnership types listed on the website:
  - CSR Partnership
  - NGO / Non-Profit Collaboration
  - Academic Partnership
  - Corporate Partnership
  - Programme Sponsorship
  - Infrastructure Development
  - Other
- How can a company or organisation initiate a partnership or CSR collaboration?
  - Click "Initiate Partnership" in the Get Involved section on the Home page (/#get-involved), or use the Contact Us page (/contact) and select "Partnership & Collaboration" or "CSR Projects".
