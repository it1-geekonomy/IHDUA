Title: Education Initiatives & Flagship Schools
Organization: IHDUA

IHDUA operates two flagship rural educational institutions:

1. Vinayaka Jnana Vidyashale (Mysore):
Located in Mysore, Karnataka, Vinayaka Jnana Vidyashale provides high quality schooling to rural, underprivileged, and marginalized children. The institution provides:
- State-of-the-art classrooms and experiential science laboratories
- Computer literacy and digital education
- Midday meal nourishment programs
- Comprehensive sports, arts, and cultural activities
- Need-based scholarships ensuring financial constraints never stop a student's education

2. Adarsha Academy (Krishnarajasagara - KRS):
Located in KRS, Mandya/Mysore district, Adarsha Academy offers an exemplary rural educational environment comparable to modern urban schools. Key highlights:
- Focus on STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Math) and digital literacy
- Nurturing critical thinking, public speaking, and ethical leadership
- Sports coaching and physical education
- Values-based education preparing students for higher secondary and college success

Over 135 rural schools have been reached through IHDUA's educational outreach and learning initiatives.
