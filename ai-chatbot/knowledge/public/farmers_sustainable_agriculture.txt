Title: Farmers Support, Sustainable Agriculture and Livestock
Organization: IHDUA
Website: /farmers

Overview:
IHDUA empowers rural smallholder farmers through practical training, bio-inputs, and sustainable farming practices to strengthen rural food security, conserve topsoil, and provide steady household incomes.

Focus Areas & Interventions:
• Sustainable Agriculture & Soil Health: Comprehensive training in soil testing, composting, vermicomposting, and reducing reliance on chemical pesticides.
• Diversified Crop Cultivation: Introducing resilient cash crops and climate-smart alternatives such as foxtail millet and turmeric.
• Livestock Care & Dairy Support: Distribution of high-yield green fodder to improve livestock health and milk yields.
• Water Management: Promotion of drip irrigation, farm ponds, rainwater harvesting, and desiltation of traditional community tanks.

Verified Impact Statistics:
• 48+ Farmers completed in-depth Soil Health training.
• 32+ Farmers participated in Turmeric Cultivation awareness programs.
• 15 Farmers successfully adopted Foxtail Millet as an eco-resilient crop.
• 3,290+ Farmers received Green Maize Fodder for livestock nutrition.
• 43 Farmer Self-Help Groups (SHGs) active in Karnataka villages.
