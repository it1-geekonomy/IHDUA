Title: Healthcare Initiatives, Awareness and Free Medical Camps
Organization: IHDUA

Free Rural Health Camps:
• Over 28 free healthcare camps organized in remote villages with 3,487+ beneficiaries treated.
• Bringing specialist physicians, pediatricians, gynecologists, and optometrists directly to rural doorsteps.
• Free diagnostic tests including blood glucose, blood pressure, BMI, and hemoglobin levels.
• Free eye checkups and distribution of prescription spectacles.
• Early cancer screening camps for rural women.
• Free distribution of essential prescription medicines for fever, respiratory illnesses, and chronic conditions.

Preventive Health & Hygiene Awareness:
• Regular community workshops on maternal health, safe childbirth practices, and infant nutrition.
• Adolescent menstrual hygiene education and distribution of sanitary napkins.
• WASH (Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene) campaigns in schools to prevent waterborne infections.
